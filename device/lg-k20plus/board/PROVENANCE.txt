LG K20 Plus board extracts: provenance and maintenance
=========================================================

These files are small, hand-maintained extracts from the vendor reference
kernel at reference/NetHunter_K20plus_arm64_Kernel-Source (LG K20 Plus,
Linux 3.18.31, Qualcomm MSM8917). They are the whole input to
generate_dts_lg.py, the same way device/j36-ultra/mvii-board/ is the whole
input to generate_dts.py.

NOTE ON THE SoC. The K20 Plus is a Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 (MSM8917), not a
MediaTek part: its modem speaks QMI over QRTR, its Wi-Fi is a Pronto
WCN3660, its PMIC is a PMI8950 over SPMI. The drivers in linux/ target
that hardware; nothing here is shared with the MTK trees except the shape
of the directories.

Files
-----
msm8917_addrs.h  SoC base addresses + IRQ lines, from
                 arch/arm/boot/dts/qcom/msm8917.dtsi
panel_lg4894.h   LGD LG4894 720x1280 DSI video-mode geometry + init, from
                 arch/arm64/boot/dts/lge/dsi-panel-lv5-lgd-lg4894-hd-video.dtsi
panel_td4100.h   Tovis TD4100 second-source geometry + init, from
                 arch/arm64/boot/dts/lge/dsi-panel-lv5-tovis-td4100-hd-video.dtsi
touch_lg4894.h   SiW LG4894 report protocol + wiring, from
                 drivers/input/touchscreen/lge/lgsic/touch_lg4894.{c,h} and
                 msm8917-lv517_gsm_us-touch.dtsi
keys_lv517.h     volume/hall key wiring, from
                 msm8917-lv517_gsm_us-misc.dtsi (+ rev-0 override)
modem_mss.h      modem (MSS/PIL) + WCNSS outline, from msm8917.dtsi
                 (pronto@a21b000) and the msm8916/q6v5 mainline shape

Maintenance rule: same as the OPPO tree -- a new LG device adds one row to
devices.sh plus its deltas here, and the generator picks them by LG_PANEL /
LG_TOUCH / LG_REV. The reference tree is read by a human, once, at that
moment -- never by the build.
